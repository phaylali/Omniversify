# Aether — The First Wanderer

In the beginning, there was only the Void. Then came Aether—the first being to walk between the dimensions, bridging the gaps that separated worlds.

## Origin

Aether emerged from the Null Space, a realm of pure potentiality. Unlike other beings who were born from the fabric of reality, Aether *created* that fabric through their mere presence.

## Abilities

- **Dimensional Walking** — Can traverse any reality without restriction
- **Temporal Vision** — Sees all possible timelines simultaneously
- **Void Touch** — Can manipulate the primordial energy of creation

## The Wanderer's Quest

Aether wanders the multiverse, seeking to understand the nature of existence. They are neither good nor evil—simply curious.

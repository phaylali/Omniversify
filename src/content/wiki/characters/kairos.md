# Kairos — Time Keeper

The guardian of temporal integrity, Kairos ensures that the flow of time remains stable across all dimensions. Little is known about this enigmatic figure.

## Nature

Kairos exists outside of normal time. They can appear at any point in history, always at the exact moment they're needed most.

## The Role of Time Keeper

As the Time Keeper, Kairos:
- Prevents temporal paradoxes
- Eliminates timeline threats
- Maintains the Temporal Accord

## Encounters

Kairos has appeared throughout history, guiding key figures at crucial moments. They appeared to the protagonist of Project Alpha, providing cryptic warnings about the dangers of temporal manipulation.

## Appearance

Kairos manifests as a figure cloaked in shifting hourglasses—each moment of their existence visible as grains of sand flowing in impossible directions.

## Quote

> "Time is not a river to be controlled. It is an ocean to be navigated. Those who try to bottle it will only find themselves drowned."

# Lyra — Queen of Shadows

The most mysterious figure in recent history, Lyra rose from obscurity to become the ruler of the Shadow Realm—and then vanished without a trace.

## Early Life

Born in the mortal realm, Lyra discovered her affinity for shadow magic at a young age. She was exiled for practicing forbidden arts, only to emerge centuries later as the Queen of Shadows.

## Reign

Under Lyra's rule, the Shadow Realm flourished. She established trade routes between the light and dark dimensions, brokered peace treaties, and became the most influential figure in inter-dimensional politics.

## The Vanishing

During the events of Project Gamma, Lyra disappeared. Her throne sits empty, and the Shadow Realm has since fallen into chaos.

## Abilities

- **Shadow Walking** — Can step into shadows and emerge anywhere
- **Mind Control** — Even the strongest wills can be swayed
- **Eternal Youth** — Lyra does not age

# Nexus War — The Great Convergence

The Nexus War was the cataclysmic conflict that reshaped the multiverse, forever altering the balance of power between the dimensional realms.

## Causes

The war began when the Seven Nations of the Prime Dimension discovered a method to tap into the energy of other realities. This threatened to destabilize the entire multiverse.

## The Battle

For a thousand years, armies from hundreds of dimensions clashed in the Nexus—a dimensional crossroads at the heart of reality itself.

### Key Figures

- **Aether** — Mediated between warring factions
- **The Null King** — Led the rebellion against the Prime Nations
- **Lyra** — Queen of Shadows, played both sides

## Aftermath

The war ended with the Great Convergence—a massive energy release that merged several dimensions permanently. From the ashes rose the Amaraith Realm (Project Beta).

## Legacy

The Nexus War established the **Treaty of Crossings**, laws governing dimensional travel and commerce that still stand today.

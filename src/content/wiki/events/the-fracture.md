# The Fracture — When Worlds Broke

The Fracture was the single most devastating event in recorded history—a catastrophic failure of dimensional integrity that destroyed entire worlds and created the shattered landscape of the Outlands.

## The Cataclysm

It began without warning. Reality simply... tore. The boundaries between dimensions collapsed, creating a chaotic region where physics, time, and logic no longer applied.

## Causes

The exact cause remains unknown, but theories include:

- **Temporal Overload** — Too many time travelers disrupting the timeline
- **Divine Conflict** — Warring gods tearing reality apart
- **Natural Entropy** — The multiverse reaching the end of its cycle

## The Aftermath

Millions were displaced as their home dimensions were consumed. The Fracture created the **Outlands**—a lawless territory where anything goes.

## Project Connections

- **Project Alpha**: Temporal Markets were established to prevent another Fracture
- **Project Gamma**: The Nullification threatens to cause a second Fracture
- **Project Delta**: The Architect's Realm exists within a stabilized pocket of the Fracture

# Void Blade

The Void Blade is one of the most feared weapons in the multiverse—a blade forged from primordial chaos that can cut through the fabric of reality.

## Origins

The first Void Blade was created by the Null King during the Nexus War. Using fragments of broken reality, he forged a weapon that could wound even the gods.

## Abilities

- **Reality Cutting** — Can slice through dimensional barriers
- **Void Energy** — Emits chaotic energy that disrupts magical protections
- **Eternal Edge** — Never dulls, never breaks

## Known Void Blades

Several Void Blades are known to exist:

1. **The Null Edge** — wielded by the Null King
2. **Shadow's Bite** — once owned by Lyra
3. **The Silent Fang** — currently unaccounted for

## Dangers

The Void Blade corrupts its wielder over time. Many who have wielded it have been consumed by the void they carry, becoming hollow shells of their former selves.

## Gameplay

In Project Gamma, players can obtain the Void Blade as a legendary weapon, though it comes with significant risks.

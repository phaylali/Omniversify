# Crystal Age — The Age of Magic

The Crystal Age was a golden era of magical prosperity that lasted over ten thousand years, when the peoples of the Prime Dimension first learned to harness the arcane energies of creation.

## The Discovery

It began with the **First Crystals**—strange crystalline formations that appeared spontaneously across the realm. These crystals contained raw magical energy, and those who learned to attune to them gained access to powers previously thought impossible.

## The Crystal Kingdoms

During this era, great kingdoms rose built upon Crystal Magic:

- **Luminar** — Masters of healing and light magic
- **Umbrhold** — Specialists in shadow and protection
- **TempestReach** — Controllers of weather and elements
- **Mindhollow** — Seers and telepaths

## The Golden Era

For ten thousand years, peace reigned. Magic lifted the burdens of daily life—disease was cured, scarcity was eliminated, and the boundaries of what was possible expanded daily.

## The End

The Crystal Age ended with the **Cascade**—a catastrophic failure of the Crystal network that sent magical energies into chaos. This event is believed to be a precursor to the Nexus War.

## Legacy

Though the Crystal Age has ended, its legacy remains. Modern magic is a shadow of what it once was, but the ancient texts still hold secrets waiting to be rediscovered.

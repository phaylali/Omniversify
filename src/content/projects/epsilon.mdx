## About

Survive the unthinkable in this psychological horror experience. Your sanity is your only weapon against the unknown entities that lurk beyond the veil of reality.

## Core Features

- **Psychological Horror** — Fear comes from within, not just without
- **Sanity System** — Your perception of reality becomes your greatest enemy
- **Environmental Storytelling** — Uncover the horror through exploration and discovery

## About

An action-adventure game spanning multiple dimensions. Fight through interdimensional threats and uncover hidden truths that challenge the very nature of reality.

## Core Features

- **Dimensional Travel** — Seamlessly shift between parallel universes
- **Dynamic Combat** — Adaptive fighting system that evolves with your playstyle
- **Branching Story** — Your choices reshape the multiverse

- **OS:** Windows 10/11, macOS 12+, Linux (Ubuntu 22.04+)

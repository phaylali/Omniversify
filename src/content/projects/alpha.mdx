## About

Project Alpha is a groundbreaking sci-fi RPG set in a universe where time is currency. Navigate the Temporal Markets and rewrite your destiny.

## Core Features

- **Time as Currency** — Buy, sell, and trade seconds, minutes, and hours
- **Temporal Combat** — Manipulate time to outsmart enemies
- **Branching Narratives** — Every choice creates new timelines

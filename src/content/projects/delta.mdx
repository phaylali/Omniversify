## About

A mind-bending puzzle platformer where gravity is your weapon. Navigate impossible architectures and solve cosmic riddles that defy physics.

## Core Features

- **Gravity Manipulation** — Bend reality to solve increasingly complex puzzles
- **Hand-Crafted Levels** — Every stage is a meticulously designed work of art
- **Metroidvania Elements** — Unlock new abilities to access previously unreachable areas

## System Requirements

- **OS:** Windows 10/11, macOS 12+, Linux (Ubuntu 22.04+)
- **Processor:** Intel Core i3-10100 or AMD Ryzen 3 3100
- **Memory:** 8 GB RAM
- **Graphics:** NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1650 or AMD Radeon RX 5500 XT
- **Storage:** 4 GB available space

Currently in **Alpha** stage. Level design in progress.

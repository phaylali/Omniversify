## About

Enter a sprawling fantasy MMORPG where ancient gods awaken. Forge alliances, build empires, and battle for cosmic supremacy in a world where mythology meets reality.

## Core Features

- **Ancient Gods** — Encounter and align with deities from every mythology
- **Massive World** — Explore a continent-spanning realm with seamless zones
- **Player Economy** — Build trade networks and dominate markets

## System Requirements

- **OS:** Windows 10/11, macOS 12+, Linux (Ubuntu 22.04+)
- **Processor:** Intel Core i7-12700K or AMD Ryzen 7 5800X
- **Memory:** 32 GB RAM
- **Graphics:** NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 or AMD Radeon RX 7800 XT
- **Storage:** 80 GB available space

Currently in **Pre-Alpha** stage. Concept and core systems in development.

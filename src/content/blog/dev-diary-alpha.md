# Dev Diary: Building Project Alpha

Welcome to our first development diary! Today, we're pulling back the curtain on **Project Alpha** and sharing some insights into our creative process.

## The Core Concept

What if time itself was the most valuable currency? This question led us to create the Temporal Markets — a complex economic system where seconds can be bought, sold, and stolen.

## Technical Challenges

Building a time-manipulation system comes with unique challenges:

1. **Visualizing Time** — How do you show temporal energy flowing?
2. **Puzzle Design** — Creating puzzles that bend but don't break logic
3. **Narrative Branching** — Ensuring every choice feels meaningful

## Early Progress

We're making strong progress on the core mechanics. The team has been experimenting with different visual styles for the Temporal Markets, and we're loving the results.

More updates coming soon!

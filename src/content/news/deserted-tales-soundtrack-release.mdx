We are thrilled to announce that the **Deserted Tales I Original Soundtrack** is now available on major streaming platforms worldwide!

Inspired by traditional Moroccan music — including **Malhun** and **Tarab al-Andalusi** — the soundtrack captures the spirit of 16th-century Morocco, blending epic orchestral arrangements with authentic North African instrumentation.

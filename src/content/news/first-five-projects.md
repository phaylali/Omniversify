Today marks a major milestone for Omniversify — we're officially announcing our first five projects!

## The Lineup

1. **Project Alpha** — Sci-fi RPG where time is currency
2. **Project Beta** — Fantasy MMORPG with ancient gods
3. **Project Gamma** — Action-adventure across dimensions
4. **Project Delta** — Puzzle platformer with gravity mechanics
5. **Project Epsilon** — Psychological horror experience

## What's Next

Each project represents a different genre and playstyle, but they're all connected through our shared multiverse lore. Characters from one project may appear in another. Events in one timeline may affect another.

We're excited to share more details about each project in the coming months!

Stay tuned!

We are excited to announce that **Warframe Tools** is now officially available on the Google Play Store! 

Warframe Tools is a free, offline-first void relic inventory manager built with Flutter, designed specifically for Warframe players who want to track their relic farming progress efficiently.

## About Warframe Tools

With Warframe Tools, you can track all **724 relics** across the five tiers:

- **Lith** - Early game relics
- **Meso** - Mid-game relics  
- **Neo** - End-game relics
- **Axi** - High-tier relics
- **Requiem** - Special end-game relics

Each relic can be tracked in four conditions: **Intact**, **Exceptional**, **Flawless**, and **Radiant**.

## Key Features

- **Offline-First Design**: All 724 relics are bundled in the app—no internet required
- **Optional Cloud Sync**: Connect to your PocketBase server for backup and sync across devices
- **Moroccan Luxury UI**: Beautiful Black & Gold design with Moon & Space mode options
- **Detailed Statistics**: Get a comprehensive breakdown of your entire relic collection
- **Natural Sorting**: Relics sort correctly (Neo Z1, Z2, Z10, Z11) with Unvaulted items prioritized
- **Fast Search**: Quickly find any relic by name or type
- **Image Preview**: Tap any relic to see its full-size visual

## Why Warframe Tools?

As Warframe players ourselves, we created this app to solve a real problem: tracking vaulted and unvaulted relics across multiple tiers and conditions. Warframe Tools makes relic farming more organized and efficient.

Download Warframe Tools on Google Play and never lose track of your farming progress again!

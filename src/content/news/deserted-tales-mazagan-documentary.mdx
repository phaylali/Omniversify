## What It Is

A complete walk-through recording of the fortress — every wall, bastion, rampart and structure, filmed slowly and without commentary. There's no music, no narration, and no fast cuts: the point is to record the place, not to make a film about it.

It was shot during the **research phase** of Deserted Tales. The fortress serves as a main point of interest in the game and the setting for its opening scenes, so the goal was to capture enough real-world detail — wall dimensions, textures, spatial relationships, architectural measurements — that the fortress can be recreated in 3D as close to life as possible, even for someone who has never been there.

## What's In It

- **The Portuguese Cistern** — the Manueline masterpiece with its five-metre pool doubling as a mirror for the cupola
- **The Governor's Bastion** — the largest of the enceinte, its upper tier blown up in 1769 and never rebuilt
- **The full rampart walk** — the circuit of the walls, seaward and landward faces, and the seam where the 1514 citadel meets the 1541 star fortress
- **The Church / Mosque** — one building, two faiths, one long occupation
- **The gates** — sea gate and land gate, and the trace of the mined passage
- **The star plan itself** — the bastioned enceinte of Benedetto da Ravenna, seen from the ground

## Where It Fits

Mazagan was built by the Portuguese beginning in **1514**, redesigned as a star fortress in **1541**, held for over 250 years as the last Portuguese stronghold in Morocco, and reclaimed by Sultan **Mohammed ben Abdallah** in **1769**. It's a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and it's where our game opens.

You can read the full architectural write-up in our wiki: **[[Wiki/Locations/Mazagan_Fortress]]** — along with the sieges of **1562** and **1769**.

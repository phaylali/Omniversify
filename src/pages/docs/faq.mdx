## About Omniversify

### What is Omniversify?

Omniversify is a Moroccan game development studio dedicated to building immersive games that reclaim history, culture, and identity. We believe in creating connected universes where stories of the Global South take center stage.

### Where is Omniversify located?

We are based in Oued Laou, Tetouan, Morocco. You can find us at:
45 Av. Al Massira, Oued Laou, Tetouan 93250, Morocco

### What games are you working on?

Our main projects include:

- **Deserted Tales I** - A stealth-action RPG set in Portuguese-occupied Mazagan
- **Deserted Tales: Origins** - A narrative-driven exploration game
- **Warframe Tools** - A free Android app for Warframe relic tracking

## Development

### How can I follow your progress?

You can follow our development journey through:

- Our Discord community
- Twitter/X
- The news section on this website

### Can I contribute to your projects?

We welcome contributions! Join our Discord to connect with the team and discuss how you can help.

### Are your games open source?

Some of our tools and utilities are open source. You can find them on our GitHub page. Our games themselves are not open source.

### What engines do you use?

We use a variety of engines:

- **Unreal Engine 5** for our main RPG titles
- **Ren'Py** for visual novels and narrative games
- **Flutter** for mobile applications
